The house floor plans retain the right-hand proposed layouts designated by the owner as the current design.
They match the A2.0 and A2.1 layouts in the previously supplied `design/floor-plans.pdf`.
The detached-unit extract uses the 2023 supplemental revision instead of the older A2.3 sheet repeated in the source sets.
The garage footprint is shown on the site plan; these sources do not provide a separate architectural drawing of that building.

Dimensions, room and opening IDs, drawing labels, revision clouds, and legends are retained.
Use written dimensions rather than scaling from a printout.
The floor-plan notes define dimensions as finished drywall dimensions or measurements from the wall centerline.
These excerpts support building reconstruction and are not a complete construction or permit set.

Each drawing region was rendered at 300 dpi and inserted into a new PDF at its original PDF size, using lossless image compression.
Only the selected pixels and new captions are included; source text layers, attachments, metadata and content outside the crops are not copied.
The drawings are therefore image-based rather than searchable vector PDFs.
Title blocks, contact details, professional seals/signatures, street/address labels and permit/application identifiers are omitted.
The site plan uses neutral “MAIN HOUSE,” “UNIT #2” and “STREET” labels in place of address text.

Cover sheets, code and energy forms, electrical/lighting layouts, sprinkler sheets and duplicated older drawings are excluded.
The structural excerpts contain construction details, not a whole-building section.
The originals remain in `design/approved plans/` under the private `SAGE_REFERENCE_ROOT`.
